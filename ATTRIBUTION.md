All 74 public `DESIGN.md` files at this revision are included. The analyses, including front matter, remain adapted upstream material. Original Agent Prompt Guide and Iteration Guide sections were removed; independently authored task prompts and a new iteration workflow replace them. The original component descriptions and visual design rules are retained as analysis, not claimed as newly authored content.

The catalog interface, Chinese catalog descriptions, 222 task prompts, general workflow prompts, preview integration, build scripts, documentation, and tests are new work by Awesome AI Web Design contributors, also under MIT.

The currently published catalog previews are actual Google Stitch generations based on the design analysis and independently written scenario prompts. Each STITCH.json records the Stitch project and screen IDs and SHA-256 checksums of the unmodified exports. STITCH-PROMPT.md records the final screen-generation request. They remain fictional product demos while the requested official-site rebuild is in progress; they must not be described as verified copies of current brand sites. Exported HTML may reference third-party fonts, image hosting, and Tailwind CDN; those services and assets retain their respective terms. No fabricated opposite-theme version is included.

The separate `.stitch-work/current-official/` working area contains public-site observations, native Stitch outputs, and, for visual comparison, some brand logos, media, and fonts from the brands' own public websites. Six working HTML pages and only their referenced assets are also served under `official-drafts/`, clearly labeled as unfinished, unofficial snapshots. None has passed complete visual or responsive review. Those third-party assets retain their owners' rights and are not covered by the MIT grant for this collection's original code and writing. The working area and official drafts are not included in `assets/all-designs.zip`.

The Linear draft also loads three mobile illustration images and its variable font from Linear's public CDN. These are Linear-owned reference assets, not part of this project's MIT-licensed work.
