All 74 public `DESIGN.md` files at this revision are included. The analyses, including front matter, remain adapted upstream material. Original Agent Prompt Guide and Iteration Guide sections were removed; independently authored task prompts and a new iteration workflow replace them. The original component descriptions and visual design rules are retained as analysis, not claimed as newly authored content.

The catalog interface, Chinese catalog descriptions, 222 task prompts, general workflow prompts, preview integration, build scripts, documentation, and tests are new work by Awesome AI Web Design contributors, also under MIT.

The currently published catalog previews are actual Google Stitch generations based on the design analysis and independently written scenario prompts. Each STITCH.json records the Stitch project and screen IDs and SHA-256 checksums of the unmodified exports. STITCH-PROMPT.md records the final screen-generation request. They remain fictional product demos while the requested official-site rebuild is in progress; they must not be described as verified copies of current brand sites. Exported HTML may reference third-party fonts, image hosting, and Tailwind CDN; those services and assets retain their respective terms. No fabricated opposite-theme version is included.

The separate `.stitch-work/current-official/` working area contains public-site observations, native Stitch outputs, and, for visual comparison, some brand logos, media, and fonts from the brands' own public websites. Forty-three working HTML pages and their referenced assets are served under `official-drafts/`, clearly labeled as unfinished, unofficial snapshots. The Airbnb page uses its previously preserved native Stitch export as a seed while a new current-site-specific Stitch request remains pending; its public imagery and content were reconstructed from a dated Canadian-site observation. The Wise Canada page includes read-only, date-stamped examples of financial rates and claims; it is not a live calculator. The Tesla page uses a dated live visual observation and directly downloaded official public media because an independent server-side HTML request returned HTTP 403. The Mistral, Replicate, Together AI, Sanity, and Sentry pages use dated current public HTML and locally adapted official stylesheets; native Stitch screens are preserved separately for comparison. Sanity's public media was captured with provenance, while its hosted draft loads large images and videos from Sanity's CDN and packages only the lightweight styles, fonts, and graphics. Sentry's 40 captured first-party assets are packaged with its static draft; its original animation and hosted interactions are not fully reproduced. The IBM Canada draft uses dated current public copy, 11 images observed in the live browser, and five IBM Plex Sans font files; its remaining captured source assets are kept for provenance and are not in the hosted build. The IBM header now uses an official IBM Design Language 8-bar SVG; its client-side menus and carousel remain incomplete. The MongoDB draft uses a current official snapshot, 24 selected public images, logos and font files, and separately preserved real Stitch outputs; its requested mobile Stitch screen was returned on a desktop canvas. None of the 43 has passed complete visual or responsive review. Third-party assets retain their owners' rights and are not covered by the MIT grant for this collection's original code and writing. The working area and official drafts are not included in `assets/all-designs.zip`.

The Linear draft also loads three mobile illustration images and its variable font from Linear's public CDN. These are Linear-owned reference assets, not part of this project's MIT-licensed work.
