[SYSTEM FAQ]

Frequently Examined Questions

[+] How does Axiom stay local without GPU dependencies?

AST indexing, syntax traversal, and heuristic patches happen via compiled C/Rust binaries in-memory. For generative tasks, Axiom connects to your local Ollama or llama.cpp daemon, or any custom API key you provision. No intermediary servers ever touch your buffers.

[+] Can I use Axiom inside Neovim or tmux?

Yes. We ship axiom.nvim (a zero-dependency Lua plugin) and standard tmux bindings. It behaves like any Unix filter utility (cat, grep, sed).

[+] What languages are supported by the AST engine?

Go, Rust, TypeScript, Python, C/C++, Zig, Java, Kotlin, Ruby, Elixir, and SQL. Tree-Sitter grammars are compiled statically directly into the single binary.
