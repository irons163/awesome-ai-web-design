In high-velocity enterprise organizations, perimeter defense topologies are inherently insufficient against sophisticated credential elevation vectors. Modern edge networks mandate deterministic, context-aware verification at every hop—enforcing cryptographically verified mutual TLS (mTLS) combined with continuous ephemeral token rotation.

This specification document outlines the operational implementation matrix across edge worker tiers, distributed key management services (KMS), and automated revocation pipelines.
