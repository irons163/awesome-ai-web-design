Chapter 01 — Engineering Monograph

The Architecture of Speed

Modena Technical Department // Aurelia Dossier

Purity of Form, Ferocity of Purpose

Developed in the heart of the Italian Motor Valley, the Aurelia GT powertrain mounts its twin titanium turbochargers inside the 90-degree cylinder bank hot-V layout. This achieves near-instant throttle latency and an explosive torque curve across the entire rev-band up to 8,200 rpm.

Engineered with dry-sump lubrication and equal-length inconel headers, acoustic frequencies are tuned deliberately for mechanical resonance that evokes classical endurance prototypes.
