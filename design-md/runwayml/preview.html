ACCREDITATION & STANDARDS

All prints preserved under ISO 18911:2010 specifications for processed safety photographic film storage. Maintained at 4°C (39°F) and 30% Relative Humidity in nitrogen-purged hermetic canisters.

OPTICAL CURATION

Optical testing conducted on collimator benches in our Zurich workshop. Lenses are calibrated for natural field curvature, un-coated flare retention, and authentic chromatic roll-off.

FESTIVAL & LOAN DESK

Physical 35mm and 70mm exhibition prints are available on loan to accredited cinematheques, archival retrospectives, and DCI-compliant screening facilities worldwide.
