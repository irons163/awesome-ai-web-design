Frequently Asked Questions

What core services does Clay provide? expand_more
Clay is a full-service digital agency. Our core offerings encompass Brand Identity & Strategy, Product Design (UI/UX), Flagship Websites & E-Commerce, Fullstack Engineering, Content & 3D Motion, and cutting-edge Generative AI workflows for consumer and enterprise brands.
How is Clay different from other design agencies? expand_more
Unlike traditional consulting firms or siloed creative shops, we fuse Silicon Valley product strategy with European high-fashion art direction. We operate in senior, cross-functional pods where designers write code and strategists prototype live interactions.
How do you handle clients across different time zones? expand_more
With studios positioned across San Francisco, New York, Austin, Denver, Lisbon, and Belgrade, we run synchronized 24-hour sprints with guaranteed working overlap for any client in North America, Europe, or Asia.
How much does a typical project cost? expand_more
Engagements are customized depending on complexity, timeline, and scope. Typical brand transformations and flagship product buildouts start from mid five-figure sprints up to comprehensive multi-phase enterprise partnerships. Reach out to our team for a tailored proposal.
Do you work with early-stage startups? expand_more
Yes. We routinely partner with high-conviction founders backed by top-tier venture firms (such as Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, and Founders Fund) to design stealth MVPs, seed-stage brand launches, and category-defining product stories.
What is your experience in complex B2B software and enterprise platforms? expand_more
Over half our portfolio consists of complex enterprise systems—including fintech rails, API developer dashboards, cloud observability software, and AI tooling. We excel at untangling massive data architectures into consumer-grade, joyful software.
