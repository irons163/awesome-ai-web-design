Why is ClickHouse so fast?

Traditional databases scan entire records horizontally. ClickHouse reads strictly the columns your query asks for, packed in compressed continuous disk blocks.

Row-oriented databases e.g. Postgres, MySQL

Reads full records including unused columns, leading to massive disk I/O penalties for analytical queries.

Row 1: [ID: 101] [User: "Alex"] [Bio: "Lorem..."] [Amount: 420.00]
Row 2: [ID: 102] [User: "Sara"] [Bio: "Ipsum..."] [Amount: 890.50]
Row 3: [ID: 103] [User: "John"] [Bio: "Dolor..."] [Amount: 112.20]
High I/O: Reads 100% of bytes to aggregate 1 column
Column-oriented databases ClickHouse

Reads strictly query-targeted columnar streams. Compact data enables vectorized SIMD instructions.

Column [Amount]: [420.00, 890.50, 112.20, ... 10M rows]
Column [User]: (Skipped from disk entirely)
Column [Bio]: (Skipped from disk entirely)
Extreme speed: Zero unnecessary disk scan, SIMD-parallel

1. Why should I use ClickHouse vs. X database? ▼

ClickHouse is specifically engineered from the ground up for analytical workloads (OLAP). While transactional databases like Postgres or MySQL prioritize concurrent row-level locks and mutations, ClickHouse compresses continuous column arrays to execute analytical aggregations (COUNT, SUM, AVG, GROUP BY) orders of magnitude faster with minimal hardware resources.

2. What is an OLAP database? ▼

OLAP stands for Online Analytical Processing. OLAP databases optimize for read-heavy operations scanning billions of rows across a few columns simultaneously, contrasting with OLTP (Online Transaction Processing) engines built for rapid single-row updates.

3. Can ClickHouse handle large volumes of data? ▼

Yes. ClickHouse powers telemetry and event systems holding hundreds of petabytes and trillions of rows across companies like Uber, Meta, and Cloudflare, handling ingestion rates exceeding millions of rows per second.

4. How can I visualize data stored in ClickHouse? ▼

ClickHouse connects seamlessly with all standard BI and observability platforms including Grafana, Apache Superset, Metabase, Tableau, Looker, and Power BI via official plugins and JDBC/ODBC drivers.

5. How much does ClickHouse cost? ▼

The open-source ClickHouse server is 100% free under the Apache 2.0 license. ClickHouse Cloud offers transparent usage-based pay-as-you-go pricing based on compute and storage consumption, beginning with a $300 trial credit.
