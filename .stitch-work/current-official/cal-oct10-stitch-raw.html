Frequently asked questions

Is Cal.com free for individuals?

Yes! Unlimited scheduling, unlimited event types, and integrations with your favorite calendar providers are 100% free for individuals.

How do calendar syncs avoid double bookings?

Cal.com connects directly to Google Calendar, Outlook, Office 365, and Apple Calendar to cross-reference all of your schedules in real time.

Can I embed Cal.com on my own website?

Yes, you can embed your booking flow as an inline iframe, modal popup, or floating button on any website using our standard embed script or React package.

What makes Cal.com different from other scheduling tools?

Cal.com is open-core, infinitely customizable, supports self-hosting, has white-label options, and provides comprehensive APIs and webhooks for developers.

Does Cal.com support team and round-robin scheduling?

Yes, our team plans include collective events, round-robin assignments based on priority or load, and managed organization routing.
