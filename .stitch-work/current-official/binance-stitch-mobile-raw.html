Frequently Asked Questions

01 What makes Binance one of the world's leading cryptocurrency exchanges?
+
Binance is the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume, trusted by over 330 million users globally. We offer industry-leading liquidity, top-tier security standards, proof of reserves, and 24/7 dedicated customer assistance.
02 Where can I buy cryptocurrency?
+
You can purchase cryptocurrencies directly on Binance via credit/debit card, bank transfer, peer-to-peer (P2P) trading, or instant crypto deposits. Visit our Buy Crypto page to get started.
03 How can I start cryptocurrency trading on Binance?
+
To start trading: 1. Register a Binance account. 2. Complete identity verification. 3. Deposit funds or crypto. 4. Navigate to Spot or Futures trading interface to execute trades.
04 Can I buy and trade cryptocurrency on the Binance app?
+
Yes, the Binance mobile app supports full account management, instant purchases, spot and futures trading, staking, Binance Earn, and Web3 Wallet integration on both iOS and Android devices.
05 How can I buy Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP and other cryptocurrencies on Binance?
+
Search for your preferred asset (such as BTC, ETH, XRP) in the Markets tab, select your preferred fiat currency or stablecoin pairing, enter the order amount, and confirm the transaction.
06 What crypto products and services does Binance offer?
+
Binance provides spot trading, margin, futures, options, Binance Earn, Launchpool, Binance Pay, NFT and Web3 marketplaces, institutional custody, and decentralized liquidity services.
07 How can I earn passive income from crypto?
+
Explore Binance Earn to discover Simple Earn (flexible and locked terms), ETH Staking, Dual Investment, and automated yield accumulation products.
08 How can I track cryptocurrency prices?
+
Visit the Markets section for real-time order books, 24-hour price charts, top gainers/losers, heatmaps, and customizable watchlists.
09 Can I trade U.S. stocks and ETFs on Binance?
+
Binance offers tokenized stock representations (bStocks) where eligible jurisdictions allow accredited digital asset trading of leading equities and indices around the clock.
10 How can I find new crypto coins early on Binance?
+
Check the Launchpool and Launchpad platforms, monitor the New Listing announcement feed, and participate in Binance Square community disclosures to discover promising early projects.
